Feature Importance Hierarchy

The Random Forest model weighs subscriber behavioral patterns to determine churn risk:

  1. Customer Service Calls (28.4%) — Strongest churn indicator. 4+ calls triggers >60% risk.
  2. Total Day Minutes (24.1%) — High usage accounts churn when billable rates exceed expectations.
  3. Total Day Charge (18.2%) — Financial impact per daytime minute.
  4. International Plan (12.5%) — Non-discounted intl usage leads to bill shock.
  5. Total Evening Minutes (8.7%) — Secondary usage behavior.
