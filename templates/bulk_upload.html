How Batch Analysis Works

  1. Upload Dataset: Provide raw customer records in CSV or XLSX format.
  2. Automatic Data Cleaning: Missing values are imputed and state codes are mapped to 69 model features.
  3. Random Forest Inference: The ML model calculates churn probabilities and risk levels for every row.
  4. BI Dashboard Insights: Real-time KPI summaries, interactive charts, and risk signals are rendered instantly.
  5. Export Predictions: Download the complete scored dataset with model output columns appended.
